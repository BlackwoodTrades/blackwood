The Build

This isn't vaporware.
Here's what we built.

Most "AI trading" products are a landing page and a promise. Ours started as working code. Here's what actually went into the engine.

01

Direct market access

The bot talks to Kalshi's API directly with cryptographically signed requests — live prices streaming in, orders going out, and an automated resolver that settles every position and logs the result. No manual steps, no spreadsheets.

02

A model for every market

Different markets need different math. Sports contracts get priced against live sportsbook odds. Crypto markets run through a log-normal probability model built on Deribit implied volatility. Fed markets get cross-referenced against Polymarket prices. One engine, multiple specialized models.

03

Risk management first

Position sizing runs on fractional Kelly (0.25×) with drawdown protection and hard dollar caps on every trade. Single-outcome markets only — no parlays, ever. The edge threshold filters out anything marginal before it becomes a trade.

04

Proven on paper before going live

Every strategy ran in dry-run mode first — full paper trading with a trade-by-trade PnL tracker before a single dollar of real capital was at risk. The same tracker grades every win and loss today, so we always know exactly how the model is performing.
